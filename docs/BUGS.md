# Bugs we have had

Moved here from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13.

Every one of these was found by the user in-game or by a game test, and has a regression test unless noted. Check this list before touching `AntNavigator`, `AntPathFinder`, the goals, or anything that places blocks.

**Pathing and climbing (the legs, `nav` package)**
1. **Route that was only the ant's own cell crashed the game.** When the ant drifted out of that cell, the walker had no next waypoint. Fix in `AntNavigator`, with a test. Lesson: a route of length 1 is a legal route.
2. **Ant went up and down one block for minutes.** It planned its route while hanging in the air above a pit. Climbing back out passed through that same cell, which the walker read as "back at the start", so it walked down and climbed again for ever. The stuck check never fired because the ant kept moving. Fix: the cell above the start of an up-and-over stretch is never read as an earlier waypoint (`cornerOf`), plus a safety net that replans after 160 ticks without a new waypoint. Lesson: "moving" is not "making progress".
3. **Ants floated up in front of a wall** before climbing over a ledge (each hill step). Now they walk to the foot of the wall first.
4. **Ants hovered at the foot of the shaft wall** with the model tilted up. They slid sideways while already counted as climbing. Now they walk to the wall and only start clinging once they touch it (only while `onGround`).
5. **Ants stuck and hovering inside the shaft** (three causes): a walk that ended in the shaft made the ant fall and re-climb about 15 times (now `holdOnToWall`); the wall was chosen cell by cell, so ants slid across the shaft in mid-air (now one wall per straight climb, `chooseWall`); an ant coming off a stair counted as "in the air" (now `standsOnFloor` checks the world). `watch` in `NavigationTests` now fails any walk where a climbing ant hangs without rising for more than 4 ticks.
5b. **Ants floated across the shaft when a new job came while they hung on a wall**: the new route wanted another wall of the cell and the ant slid straight to it. Now it crawls round the corners (`crawlAim`). Lesson: any change of wall while clinging must go over the walls.
6. **Ants never cross the open shaft** and must get against a wall before rising. Keep this when adding new moves. (Since surface walking this is built in: no move of the planner crosses open air.)
6b. **A body a hair over the edge of its cell catches on the next block** (surface walking, found by the thin-soil test: the wide queen stuck for good going over the edge into the stairs, workers now and then on a climb or crawl). The collision checks the up/down move first, while the body still pokes 0.003 blocks into the neighbouring column. Fix: `moveAttached` makes tiny corrections first, on their own. Lesson: exact motions next to blocks must keep the box inside its cell.
6c. **A motion smaller than about 0.0003 blocks is thrown away by the game** (`Entity.move` ignores it), so an ant waiting for a tiny correction waited for ever (thousands of stuck steps in one test run). Corrections under 0.001 are now skipped (still inside the cell's 0.002 margin). And any move along a surface that gets nowhere for 20 ticks counts as blocked: the legs remember that foothold for 20 seconds and plan round it (`AntNavigator.isBlocked`, `avoid`, a `StepCosts` of the legs themselves).

6d. **The body spun half round going over a thin wall** (found with `tools/chimney_preview.py`, the chimney's rim): the client tipped the body up onto the new wall from its body yaw, which had already turned toward that wall, and a body not yet flat turned its wall yaw toward the opposite wall through 180 degrees. Now the tip starts from the last tick's body yaw (`floorYaw`) and onto a wall facing the other way the body lies flat first (`AbstractAntEntity.turnBody`). Lesson: the client gets position, yaw and the synced data only every 3 ticks; draw motions from what the body looked like, not from the newest number.

6e. **Crash: a chimney mouth turned into a spire piece under a termite** (user's crash report, 2026-10-13, several termites peeking out at once on a growing mound): the builders reshaped a mouth into an ordinary hollow spire piece (the spire grew taller) while a termite was going in by it; the walker read the block as a doorway without checking and the game crashed (`ClassCastException` in `AntNavigator.intoMouth`). The per-tick check missed it because a spire piece is still a channel. Now `AntPathFinder.whatChanged` also checks that a mouth step's block is still a mouth (the termite plans again), and `intoMouth`/`outOfMouth` never cast blindly. Unit test `aMouthReshapedIntoASpirePieceIsNoLongerAWayInOrOut`. Confirmed in-game by the user. Lesson: anything the builders can reshape can change under an insect in the middle of a move.

6f. **Termites stood on top of the mound in daylight** (user's report with a picture, 2026-10-13: "they all get stuck at the top trying to place the bottom of the spire" going from stage 13 to 14). Read from the user's save (`tools`-less: a small NBT reader in the session scratchpad): the main spire's bottom piece had been cleared (stage 14 wants a tunnel there, the spire moves up a block), the two pieces above it hung in the air for now, and termites stood in that empty cell and in empty cells of the tier below. `Nest.isInside` counted every cell the plan makes a tunnel, spire piece or mouth as "inside" even while it was still open air, so by day they thought they were in and stayed. Now such a cell is inside only once a channel block is really there. Game test `termitesFinishTheUsersHalfBuiltStageFourteen` puts the user's own half-built mound (dumped from the save into `data/antcolonies/gametest_user_mound_14.txt`, same shape seed via `Mound.setSeedForTests`) round a test colony, with night and day taking turns, and checks stage 14 gets finished; `termitesGrowStageThirteenIntoFourteen` does the same from a stage 13 put up at once. Note: the game test world has random ticks off, so wet soil never sets there by itself: a test that needs it must set it (the user-mound test does it at about the game's pace). Confirmed in-game by the user.

6g. **Termites buried in a spire piece** (found by `termitesBuildTheYoungCathedralFromInside` after the "reshape in place" fix, 2026-10-13): a spire piece filled up into solid soil while termites were crawling up its channel walled them in for good (three builders idle in one cell for 70,000 ticks, no foothold, so the young cathedral was never finished). Now a piece with anyone in it is filled up into a tunnel block instead (the channel stays; it then stays a tunnel block where the plan has soil, which only adds a way through), a termite standing on it outside the channel is lifted out, and a player or animal in it stops the job (`Mound.shape`). Lesson: like `placePellet` and `addLayer`, anything that fills a cell must deal with who is in it, including insects inside channels.

**Several ants, one cell (ants do not collide)**
7. **Hill last spots deadlocked** because several ants stood in the same spot. Ants now prefer free spots and step aside before placing.
8. **Hill jam with 4 to 12 ants in one gap** (user's report, found with the `[pellet]` log). All ants with soil walked to the same spot, which lies where every ant leaving the hole passes, so someone was always inside and `placePellet` (refuses while anyone is in the cell) never got a turn. Fix: one claim per spot (`moundClaims`), the soil nudges ants out onto the new block (`nudgeOut`), and a player or animal in the spot still stops it. Lesson: any rule of the form "do X only if the cell is empty" will fail for ever where traffic is dense.
9b. **Sealers shut themselves in** (surface walking, `workersCloseEveryHoleInTheNestWalls`: 4 or 5 workers stuck for good in a crack). The test's crack runs through the seed store and the nursery; a hole between them could be closed from the nursery cell below it, which was only reachable through that very hole (the new climbing made it reachable). Up to 4 ants went through, packed the hole above themselves and were shut in. Now a sealer goes to the first spot it can reach without passing through the hole (`NestWalls.plugSpots`, `SealGoal.walkToAPlugSpot`, the walk forbids the hole with `StepCosts.forbid`), and a digger only stands inside the nest's own space, at the entrance or above ground (`Nest.standSpotsFor`), never down a crack beside the nest. Lesson: never let a job put an ant on the far side of something it or another job is about to close.
9c. **Route started from a floor that isn't there** (surface walking): an ant standing at the rim of a hole counts as on the ground although the block under its middle is open, so routes started from a floor foothold that does not exist, failed on their first tick, and were planned again for ever (12765 give-ups in one test run). Now the start is worked out from the body: its wall, its floor only if it is really there, else the floor of the cell beside it that it stands on (`AntNavigator.currentFoothold`, `floorBeside`).
9. **A pellet once buried the queen.** Pellets are never placed on a living creature (players and animals still block it).

**Nest shape and soil**
10. **Ants stopped once the shaft was dug** in a world where only the top 3 blocks were dirt and the room layer was full of stone. Now the layout is planned before the first block is dug (`chooseLayout`), with fewer levels or one block higher where the soil is thin. Ants never dig stone.
11. **A sealed-off pocket next to a block stopped the dig.** `DigGoal` now tries every open side of a block as a place to stand.
12. **Holes in the nest walls** (caves, cracks, a gap between two rooms) and **a hole right next to the top of the shaft** that nobody could reach: fixed by `SealGoal` / `getLeaks` and `findPlugSpot` (floors first, then walls).
13. **Water and lava defence** got too complicated and was removed (commits ad0fcf4 to ec5253c). It is still in git history. Hazards stay a later item.

**Colony logic**
14. **A queen could have starved with a full bread store** (caught in design): her 5-day clock only runs while she wants food and gets none; it resets while she carries an egg, has her full meal, or the nursery holds 10 young.
15. **A full larva waiting for a worker slot must not starve** (same idea: the clock only runs for a larva that still has to eat).
16a. **Nest plan re-guessed after a reload** (code review): the layout was not saved, and seed/bread heaps made the old direction look blocked, so a second nest was started. Now saved. Lesson: anything decided once from the world must be saved, because the world changes. Game tests never reload unless a test does `Colony.load(colony.save())`.
16. **Old saves:** ants and colonies from before a change must still load. New saved fields use a "not set" marker (`Colony.NOT_SET`) and old keys are still read (`queenSeeds`). Existing nests keep their old shape; new layouts only apply to new colonies.
16b. **A "once a second" timer that never ran** (found in step 6b): timers that start at `Long.MIN_VALUE` and check `now - last >= 20` overflow on the first call (`now - Long.MIN_VALUE` is negative), so the check never happens. `ForagingHoles.isAnyoneOutside` always said "nobody" because of it. Start such a check with `last == Long.MIN_VALUE ||`, or test it.

**Test-only traps**
17. **A neighbouring test's leftover colony** was found by the `colony(helper)` helper (a colony with no living queen). The helper now requires a living queen. New tests shift the arena positions, so this can come back.
17b. **Test ants joined a neighbouring test's colony** (chimney animation session: `aLoneQueenFoundsHerColonyAndRaisesHerFirstWorkers` failed because 4 worker ants from walking tests nearby had joined her colony, so she was no longer founding alone and never sealed herself in). A worker with no colony joins the nearest one within 32 blocks on its first tick, and the tests run side by side; adding one test moved the arenas so the walking tests landed near her. Now every ant a test walks by hand is spawned with `TestAnts.loneAnt` (`WorkerAntEntity.keepOutOfColonies`). Use it for any new test ant that must not belong to a colony. The reload tests make their copy of the ant mindless and colonyless too (a copy with a mind wandered off the wall it was meant to hold).
18. **Timing flakes under load:** `antsOnBumpyGroundReachTheFoodStore`, `aStarvingWorkerSlowsDownAndDies`, `queenIsFedAndHerEggGoesToTheNursery` (a nurse can pick up the egg in the very tick it is laid). Re-run once before assuming a real bug.
18b. **`workersCarrySeedsToTheFoodStore` lost one seed of five** in 2 of 5 runs during the mound-block session (the counts ended at 4, nobody holding anything, no seed item in the test area); NOT explained yet. It started after the ceilings, so ceiling wandering is a suspect but not proven. Diagnostics added: the failure message lists every ant holding a seed or bread and every item within 64 blocks (`carriers`), `StoreSeedGoal` logs `[seed] ... dropped it` and the feeding goals log `[bread] ...`. Also fixed on the way: a nurse with bread it could take nowhere deleted it; now it puts it down where it stands as a bread heap (`Bakery.putDownBreadWhereItStands`). Next time it fails, read the message before guessing.
18c. **`termitesSealOpeningsInTheirNestWithMoundSoil` cut a "wall" that later became a doorway** (found in the step 6b full run): it picked a garden side wall that no garden dug *so far* used, but the middle of a garden's wall is where the doorway to a later garden goes, so the cut was later part of the nest and was never sealed. Now it cuts a wall cell beside a corner of the room, outside every garden the plan may ever have.
18d. **A colony's reach is bigger than a test arena** (2026-10-13, food finding session): a neighbouring test's termites cut a test's grass (pieces went missing from its count), workers of other tests' colonies wandered in and kept its alarm at 2 (no foraging, a plant never cut), and a worker put down by a test joined the nearest colony, a neighbour's. Workarounds for now: `FoodMap.setTestArea` and `Alarm.setTestArea` (set by `TermiteMoundTests.colony`), `knowForTests` in tests not about finding, `joinColony` for a test's own new worker. The real fix is spacing the arenas (see Testing tips). `aLarvaThatIsNotFedForTwoDaysStarves` (an ant test) failed in 2 full runs this way and passes alone (again once on the 65 x 65 arenas, 2026-10-14, `0 bodies, expected 1`; passes alone; not looked into yet).
18e. **A check that holds "for good" after an event** (chicken test, 2026-10-13): it asserted no foraging for the rest of the test once the alarm was up, but the alarm rightly calms after 30 seconds; when the test ran longer it "failed". Check a rule only while its cause still holds.
19. **A test that passes on the old code proves nothing** (`manyAntsWithPelletsAndOneGapDoNotJam` never failed before the hill-jam fix). Prefer a test that is first shown to fail.

20. **Termites crowding the middle hole after a chicken alarm** (user's report 2026-10-13, night, a young mound: they clung to the walls of the shaft over the hole and kept "trying to place a block" until the alarm calmed down, about 2 minutes). At alarm 2 the colony stays in, so the middle hole is plugged; two causes found in the code: (a) the mound's hollow shaft counted as inside even while a young mound has no roof over it, so foragers coming home stopped in it, above the plug; (b) `ForagingHoles.plug` refused while any creature stood in the hole, and the hole is where everybody coming home passes (the same jam as bug 8). Fixes: a mound shaft cell open to the sky is outside (`Nest.isInside`); coming-home termites never stop in a doorway (`GoInsideGoal`, `ForagingHoles.isDoorway`); nestmates in the hole are nudged down into the nest when it is plugged (anything else still stops it). The game test `TermiteAlarmTests.theMiddleHoleIsPluggedSoonAfterAChickenAlarm` could NOT recreate the jam (it passes on the old code too, see 19), so the user's retest is the real check. Lesson: a doorway must never count as a place to stop, and "inside" must mean out of the open air.

21. **Dead termites left no body in-game** (user's report 2026-10-14, punched in creative) though the game test passed: the body code needed a colony, and a termite of no colony (spawn egg far from a queen) died like a vanilla mob. Now every dead worker and soldier leaves a body. Lesson: a test spawns its insects next to a queen, the user often does not; test the colonyless case too.

22. **`theMiddleHoleIsPluggedSoonAfterAChickenAlarm` failed 1 run in 3** (first full run on the 65 x 65 arenas, 2026-10-14): not the termites but the test. It clears the plan's tunnel block over the middle hole to make a young open mound, and the builders sometimes put it back before the plug; a covered middle hole leads only into the mound's tunnels and by design is not plugged. Now the test accepts plugged or roofed over. Still a rare flake (1 in 13 alone): `the chicken is not dead yet`; the message now says whether the chicken ever came. Lesson: a test that undoes part of the plan must expect the builders to redo it.
22b. **Two more test races found the same night** (full runs on the 65 x 65 arenas): `aWorkerFindsAHayBale...` failed when the finder walked home before the colony's 5-second plant scan had seen the newly put down hay (the test now calls `FoodStore.rescanPlantsSoon`); `minorsBiteAChickenThatHunts...` asked every minor to feel the alarm, but the first one can kill the chicken before the drumming reaches the others (now at least one). Neither was a bug in the insects. Lesson: under a full run's load a test sees timings it never sees alone; check what it asks for is really what the insects must do.

**How to find the next one fast**
- Ask the user for F3 coordinates and `run\logs\latest.log`; add a one-line `[tag]` log where an ant gives up, with the spot, the ant's cell, the navigator state and what is in the spot. That found the hill jam in one round.
- Most pathing bugs come from one of: the start cell is in the air, the goal cell is where traffic passes, the route has one step, or "done" is decided from a cell instead of from real progress.
