## The real trees
- **Umbrella thorn** (*Vachellia tortilis*): the flat-topped acacia of the African
  savanna; a short trunk forking low into a few thick, twisting limbs; a flat,
  layered, see-through crown of tiny leaflets; paired long straight white spines and
  small hooked thorns; grows 20 to 50 years to full size, lives 150+.
- **Camel thorn** (*Vachellia erioloba*): darker, rounder, denser crown; very deeply
  cracked dark grey bark; thick grey velvety half-moon seed pods; very long-lived.
- **Baobab** (*Adansonia digitata*): a huge bottle-shaped trunk (up to about 10 m
  across), short stubby branches like roots in the air, leafless much of the year;
  old ones hollow while still alive (often with an opening in the trunk); spongy,
  watery wood: a dead baobab does not leave a log, it rots from inside and slumps
  into a heap of fibres within months.
- **Death:** old age, fire, lightning, drought, elephants; some termites hollow the
  heartwood of living trees from the roots up. Dead trees stand for years, shed
  their limbs, then snap or tip over. Old trunks are hollowed by heart-rot fungi long
  before they fall; then beetle larvae, termites and rot carve galleries.

## The scale (the user's pick: "Huge")
One block is about 2.3 cm to an insect, so real trees cannot fit; "Huge" is the
compromise: a mature umbrella thorn has a trunk **4 to 5 blocks thick, about 30 to
40 tall, a flat crown 40 to 50 wide**. Camel thorn a bit shorter and rounder, baobab
trunk thicker (my pick: 8 to 12 across) with a smaller crown. Too big for
Minecraft's normal tree features (they may only write about 16 blocks from their
middle), so new land places them as **structures** (pieces that may span several
chunks).

## Shared idea: one shape maker, every age
Every tree is one record (kind, place, its own dice seed, age, health, stage) kept
in the world's saved data. Its shape is worked out from the record: the same seed
at a greater age gives the same tree, bigger. So new land, growing in play, dying
and falling all use the same shape maker, and growing a stage only places the
blocks that differ. Never grows into a player's blocks (only air, grass, leaves of
its own). Tree kinds are data files (`data/antcolonies/tree_kind/*.json`: sizes,
ages, crown shape, bark and leaf blocks, lifespan, death chances), like the species
profiles, so a new tree kind later is a new file plus its textures.

## Steps
0. **Looks first, in the model viewer. DONE 2026-10-18** (`python tools/model_viewer.py trees`; the picks are in `docs/DESIGN.md`). All three kinds at each age (seedling,
   young thorny tree, mature, ancient), the dead stages (standing snag with peeling
   bark, termite mud climbing it, limbs shed, the fallen hollow giant cut open to
   show inside, the baobab heap) and the new blocks, two or three looks each. The
   user picks; nothing goes into the game before.
1. **The blocks. BUILT 2026-10-18** (`registry/TreeBlocks`, files by `python tools/tree_art.py`, `docs/HISTORY.md`). Bark and wood per kind (living), dead bark in the bleached silver
   look, peeling bark, rotten wood (soft, insects dig it fast), frass (insect wood
   dust, a thin layer on floors), leaves per kind (fine, see-through, flat layers),
   thorny twigs (hurt and slow like a sweet berry bush; at crown edges and on young
   trees, none on big old trunks), baobab fibre heap, camel thorn pods (looks only
   for now). Textures, models, loot, tags (which ones termites may eat, which ones
   insects may dig).
2. **The shape maker. BUILT 2026-10-18** (`tree/TreeShapes`, kinds in `data/antcolonies/tree_kind/`, real growth, `docs/HISTORY.md`). Shapes from the record for every kind and age; checks in
   `./gradlew test`: every block joined to the trunk, nothing floating, sizes inside
   the picked ranges, the same seed always the same tree, a bigger age only adds.
   Shapes shown in the model viewer again for the user.
3. **New land.** A biome modifier removes Minecraft's savanna trees from savannas
   (land already explored keeps its trees); our trees placed as structures with
   the real age spread (many young, some mature, a few ancient giants; my picks for
   how many), spaced like a real savanna, and every dead stage scattered in.
4. **Growing in play.** Seedlings come up near grown trees now and then; trees
   grow stage by stage slowly (seedling to young tree in a few in-game days, mature
   in about 20 to 40, ancient after 100+); bone meal speeds up only the young
   stages; time while away is caught up on return (like the builders' time,
   `BuildLife`), out of sight.
5. **Death and the fall.** Old age by chance; fire on the trunk; lightning; a
   Macrotermes colony living under a tree slowly hollows its heartwood, so it dies
   sooner. The dead tree stands as a snag; termite mud climbs it from the base; over
   several in-game days it drops its limbs as fallen branches; then the trunk falls
   in one moment (crash, dust, falling leaves, hurts what is under it) and lies as
   the hollow log; the stump stays. Inside: a central hollow sized by the tree's age,
   rotten wood and frass on the floor, side galleries, darker deeper in. Baobabs
   instead sag and slump into a fibre heap that dries and shrinks.
6. **Old dead wood folded in.** Our trees shed the fallen branches (today's
   `BranchShedding` moves to them); the 1-block fallen trees (`FallenTreeFeature`)
   replaced by fallen young trees and fallen giant limbs; dead wood takes the silver
   look. Termites eat all the new dead wood the same way as now (from below, mud
   casts, mud over wood), checked on the huge logs. Blocks already in old worlds stay.
7. **Tests and speed.** A new game test category `trees` (shapes in new land,
   growth, catch-up while away, the fall, termites eating a fallen giant); time the
   world generation and the growing in the stopwatch (`/antcolonies perf`); the
   `termitefood` and `away` categories rerun.
