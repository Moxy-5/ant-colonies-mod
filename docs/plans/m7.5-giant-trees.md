## The real trees
- **Umbrella thorn** (*Vachellia tortilis*): the flat-topped acacia of the African
  savanna; a short trunk forking low into a few thick, twisting limbs; a flat,
  layered, see-through crown of tiny leaflets; paired long straight white spines and
  small hooked thorns; grows 20 to 50 years to full size, lives 150+.
- **Camel thorn** (*Vachellia erioloba*): darker, rounder, denser crown; very deeply
  cracked dark grey bark; thick grey velvety half-moon seed pods; very long-lived.
- **Baobab** (*Adansonia digitata*): a huge bottle-shaped trunk (up to about 10 m
  across), short stubby branches like roots in the air, leafless much of the year;
  old ones hollow while still alive (often with an opening in the trunk); spongy,
  watery wood: a dead baobab does not leave a log, it rots from inside and slumps
  into a heap of fibres within months.
- **Death:** old age, fire, lightning, drought, elephants; some termites hollow the
  heartwood of living trees from the roots up. Dead trees stand for years, shed
  their limbs, then snap or tip over. Old trunks are hollowed by heart-rot fungi long
  before they fall; then beetle larvae, termites and rot carve galleries.

## The scale (the user's pick: "Huge")
One block is about 2.3 cm to an insect, so real trees cannot fit; "Huge" is the
compromise: a mature umbrella thorn has a trunk **4 to 5 blocks thick, about 30 to
40 tall, a flat crown 40 to 50 wide**. Camel thorn a bit shorter and rounder, baobab
trunk thicker (my pick: 8 to 12 across) with a smaller crown. Too big for
Minecraft's normal tree features (they may only write about 16 blocks from their
middle), so new land places them as **structures** (pieces that may span several
chunks).

## Shared idea: one shape maker, every age
Every tree is one record (kind, place, its own dice seed, age, health, stage) kept
in the world's saved data. Its shape is worked out from the record: the same seed
at a greater age gives the same tree, bigger. So new land, growing in play, dying
and falling all use the same shape maker, and growing a stage only places the
blocks that differ. Never grows into a player's blocks (only air, grass, leaves of
its own). Tree kinds are data files (`data/antcolonies/tree_kind/*.json`: sizes,
ages, crown shape, bark and leaf blocks, lifespan, death chances), like the species
profiles, so a new tree kind later is a new file plus its textures.
