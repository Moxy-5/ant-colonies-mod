- Surface walking, the climbing rewrite (user's decision, CONFIRMED in-game by the user "works absolutely perfectly"; it replaces the old cell-by-cell climbing and its patches `chooseWall`, `crawlAim`, `holdOnToWall`). User: climbing must look natural (no sliding sideways, no 180-degree spins, no floating), it must keep the same intelligence and be a better base for later minds (marches, scouting, raids), and **ants climb down head first** instead of dropping. Design: an insect always holds a surface, a **foothold** (`nav/Foothold`: a cell plus the surface in it, the floor or one of the 4 walls; ceilings later). The planner (`AntPathFinder`) searches over footholds, and every step of a **route** (`nav/Route`, immutable, shareable) says which surface and what kind of move (`nav/StepKind`): WALK, ONTO_WALL / OFF_WALL (floor to wall and back at the foot of a wall), CLIMB_UP, CLIMB_DOWN (head first), CRAWL_SIDEWAYS, TURN_CORNER (inside corner), ROUND_EDGE (outside edge), OVER_TOP (up and over onto a block), OVER_EDGE (off a floor's edge and down the side), FALL (only where nothing can be climbed down, at most 12 blocks). No move crosses open air, so a route can never float. Costs about 1 per block (climbing 1.1 to 1.2, corners 0.5 to 0.6, over an edge 1.5, a fall 3 + its height). A route into a cell with a floor ends on the floor (never hanging at the foot of a wall); in a shaft it ends on a wall and the insect holds on there. The walker (`AntNavigator`) does nothing by guessing: walking on floors uses the normal walking with gravity; every move on a wall or round an edge is an exact motion along the surface, the body pressed against it (`moveAttached`, `wallPoint`, `pinned`; first it squares the body up inside its cell, because a body a hair over the cell edge caught on the next block and stuck for good: that was the queen's bug in the thin-soil test). Climbing speed = the speed attribute x 0.24 (a worker 0.06 blocks a tick). The legs check every tick that the step is still possible (the foothold it holds, the one it goes to and any cell it passes on the way, `AntPathFinder.passesThrough`) and plan again at once when a block was put in the way (the hill growing, a hole sealed, a player); a move that gets nowhere for 20 ticks counts as blocked and that foothold is avoided for 20 seconds. A mind can give its own costs for one walk (`moveTo(goal, speed, StepCosts)`, kept when the route is planned again; `StepCosts.forbid(cells)` rules cells out): the sealers use it so they never pass through the hole they close.
