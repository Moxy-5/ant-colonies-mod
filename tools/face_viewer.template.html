Otter faces

The otter's moods: the face (ears, eyelids, eyes, whiskers, jaw, tongue, a head tilt), the tail, and how it holds its body. Three new ear designs to try on every mood. Pick a mood to see it on one otter (it eases from the last face over half a second, as it will in the game), or show all of them side by side. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to slide.

  • In the game the face is added on top of whatever the otter is doing (walking, swimming, eating, playing), the way its head already turns to look at things. Here it sits on the idle clip.
  • Excited uses the playful face. Where a clip moves a face part itself (the jaw biting or chewing, the eyelids when asleep), the clip wins on that part.
  • The body stance (crouching, leaning, the neck, the lifted paw) shows while the otter stands still; moving, its walk or bound carries the body, and the face and tail still show. Real otters hardly signal with the tail (it is a rudder and a prop to stand on), so the tail moods are kept small.
  • Its own little life: blinks at its own pace for each mood, ear flicks, ears turning about when wary, whiskers quivering when curious.
