Papyrus and hippo grass for the savanna's rivers and oxbows (docs/plans/papyrus-and-hippo-grass.md), placed by the plan's rules: papyrus stands 3 to 5 blocks tall in water 1 deep or on mud at the waterline of still and slow water; hippo grass floating out 1 to 3 blocks from the bank, rooted in clumps at the edge. Only on the slow mid and near-coast rivers (none on the fast far-inland ones) and round oxbows. For scale: the grey box is a hippo (4 long), the olive one a crocodile (7.5 long), the blue one a player (1.8 tall). Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to slide.
