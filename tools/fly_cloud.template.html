Darting and hovering

Each fly zips to a new spot every fifth to half a second, overshoots a little, stops dead and hovers with a small wobble, often turning on the spot.

Wing buzz

The see-through wings swap between two angles every frame, so they read as a blur. Landed flies fold them back in a V over the body.

Still fly

One landed fly on a stone away from the cloud, turning slowly, to judge the paint up close: the eyes, the sheen, the wings.

Landing

Now and then a fly lands on the bank for a few seconds, shuffles round and often rubs its front legs together before taking off again.
