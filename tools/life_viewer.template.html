River life cycle viewer

Each river animal's male and female side by side, its young, its eggs and its nest, on a savanna riverbank (land, a shallow shelf of water, then the deep bed). Pick an animal below. Everything is made from the game's own models with small changes; the poses are still, the courtship and care moves come with each animal's step. A player and a block stand by for scale. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
