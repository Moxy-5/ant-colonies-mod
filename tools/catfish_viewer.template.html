River catfish viewer

The river's big hunter in three looks, A to C, over a silt bed with the game's algae and a school of the game's tilapia (at their game size, 0.6 blocks) for scale. Its body is a chain of bones (head, front, middle, rear, hind, tail fin), so it swims in a long wave; its four pairs of barbels and its lower jaw are their own bones, so the whiskers sway and "Strike" makes it lunge with its mouth open. The size slider sets how long the catfish is. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
