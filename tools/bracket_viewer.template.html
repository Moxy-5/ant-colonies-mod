Bracket Fungus on the Trees

Heart rot fungi eat a tree's core while it stays green. Their hard woody shelves on the bark show the rot is well along. In the game, 2 brackets come out about 6 days after the rot gets in, and 4 once the trunk is hollow (from day 16). They stay on when the tree dies, bleached or burnt, and drop off when the wood behind them goes. The bracket is the game's own block model, drawn here on each kind of trunk.
