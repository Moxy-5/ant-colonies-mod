Fungus Comb Ages

Real comb is a conveyor belt: fresh dark pellets go on top, the fungus ripens the middle and sprouts its white nodules there, and the bottom turns pale and crumbly for the old workers to eat. Each layer of a comb block shows its own age, so a thick comb is dark on top and pale underneath. Pick the look you like.
