Floodplain Trees

The sycamore fig and the doum palm, rebuilt from your two pictures, now at every age from seedling to your grown tree ("Every age" stands them side by side, youngest on the left), and dead in three stages ("Every dead stage" shows the grown tree, then the same tree dying). Trunks, stems and thick limbs are full blocks; the thin limb ends, the roots at the foot, the fruit and every palm frond are sculpted pieces (small boxes, as a block model can hold). Nothing here is in the game yet. "Another tree" shows how much one kind differs from seed to seed. The little figures at the foot are for scale: a player, and a Matabele major ant beside them.
