Floodplain Trees

The sycamore fig and the doum palm, rebuilt from your two pictures. Trunks, stems and thick limbs are full blocks; the thin limb ends, the roots at the foot, the fruit and every palm frond are sculpted pieces (small boxes, as a block model can hold). Nothing here is in the game yet. "Another tree" shows how much one kind differs from seed to seed. The little figures at the foot are for scale: a player, and a Matabele major ant beside them.
