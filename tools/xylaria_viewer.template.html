Xylaria on a Dead Comb

Living termites keep their fungus garden clean. Once the colony is dead, the black fungus Xylaria, which was in the comb all along, takes over within days: first a grey-white fuzz of threads, then slender black stalks, single or forked like little antlers, their tips powdered pale with spores. The comb under them turns black, goes soft and crumbles to dust, leaving the garden room empty. Pick the look you like.
