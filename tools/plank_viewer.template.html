Planks from the Savanna Trees

Each giant tree's wood saws into planks of its own. Umbrella thorn boards are red-brown with a wide cream sapwood; camel thorn, one of Africa's hardest woods, is dark red to purplish with grain that ripples across the board; baobab wood is pale, soft and stringy. Pick one look for each tree: the scene shows a floor, a wall and the log they came from.
