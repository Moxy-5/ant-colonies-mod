The Matabele Prey Pile

A Matabele sting paralyses a termite. The ants carry it home still alive and eat it fresh; real nests keep no store. So in the queen's room lies a heap of living, still Macrotermes workers (pale, with golden heads) and soldiers (big orange-brown heads, black jaws), their legs and antennae flicking now and then. Each bunch brought in adds a layer. Uneaten for most of a day, they die, go still and grey-brown, and are carried out with the eaten leftovers to a heap by the entrance. Pick the look you like.
