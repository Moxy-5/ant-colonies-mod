Where could a Matabele colony live?

Every home choice, cut open down the middle, with the ants at today's real sizes: a major is half a block long (1 block = 30 mm of ant), a minor a quarter, the queen 0.6, a termite worker 0.15. White grubs are larvae, brown ones cocoons, upside-down termites the prey. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide. The buttons jump to each home. Red boxes are the blocks the ants would have to dig or clear to move in. Planning only: none of this is in the game.
