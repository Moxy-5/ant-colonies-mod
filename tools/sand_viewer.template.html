River sand, pebbles and the crocodile's nest

Rivers sort their sand: coarse red-ochre and pebbly far inland, golden mid inland, pale and fine near the coast. The sand bank shows one tone on the inside of a bend: wet sand at the waterline, ripples on the shallow bed, two crocodile slides into the water, a basking crocodile, a covered nest and a dug-open one beside it. Pebbles lie where real ones do: thickest at the bar's upstream end (the left, where the water hits it) and along the deep channel, fewer toward the coast. "Everywhere" is the heavy case, a pebble on every sand block, to judge the cost. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to slide.
