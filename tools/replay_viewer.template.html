Replay

The game's own recording of a film scene, played back: every animal exactly as the game drew it, moment by moment, on the ground the game recorded (squares a block wide, so a sliding foot shows). Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to slide; click an animal to read its clips and its mind (click the ground to let it go). Keys: space plays, the arrows step a moment (with Shift ten), F follows the picked animal, Esc lets it go. The page's address always holds the moment, the animal and the camera: copy it to come back to exactly this view.
