Hippo: the brief

Step 1 of the workshop: agree the size and shape before any model. Measured from references/inspiration/hippo.png.

Hippopotamus (M9 river zones & savanna life; first animal in the Upright Four Legs family, 2026-11-21). Blended from the user's two inspiration sheets: cleaner stepped silhouette, head, eyes and sturdy pillar legs from hippo_smooth.jpg; wide barrel top view, deep open-mouth interior (tongue + tusks) and mud-and-terracotta-pink palette from hippo_blocky.jpg.

  • Size (the user's call): 5.0 blocks long × 2.0 blocks tall (at the back dome, ~2.0–2.1 blocks wide across the barrel) for a grown bull (cows ~0.86 of that, calves ~0.36). Note: the AI side views are drawn slightly taller/stockier (~2.0:1 length-to-height); our outline uses the user's 2.5:1 (80 × 32 model px) so it reads long, heavy and real in Minecraft while keeping the exact silhouette and face proportions.
  • Cube size: built at 16 model px = 1 block (80 px long at scale 1.0), using clean 2–3 px stepped tiers (matching hippo_smooth.jpg) rather than noisy 1-px micro-voxels.
  • Key features: massive domed barrel torso with dusty-pink underbelly; wide rectangular muzzle with raised nostril bumps and darker mottled nose pad; high-set brow ridges with crisp Minecraft-style eyes (white glint) and small pink-lined ears aligned on the top waterline plane (so eyes, nostrils and ears poke out together when submerged); massive hinged lower jaw built for a 140°+ threat yawn showing four chunky ivory tusks and a thick pink tongue; four thick pillar legs with pale ivory toenails; short flat paddle tail.
  • Box budget: large animal budget 400 boxes (body_plan.py); target ~180–260 boxes.
  • Bones: root, body (hips, chest), neck, head (ear_left, ear_right, eye_left, eye_right, lid_left, lid_right, jaw, tongue), tail_1, tail_2, and four 3-bone upright legs (l1/r1/l2/r2: upper, lower, foot).
