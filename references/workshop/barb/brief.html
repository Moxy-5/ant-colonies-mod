Barb: the brief

Step 1 of the workshop: agree the size and shape before any model. Measured from references/inspiration/barb.png.

River barb (Claude's design, 2026-11-20; the user: "I let you design the small fish"). Plan: docs/plans/prey-fish-and-frogs.md.

  • Length: made 30 model pixels long (as the tilapia is made about 28), drawn in the game at 0.25 blocks; each fish 0.85 to 1.15 of that.
  • Cube size: one model pixel; the far look past 12 blocks is a handful of boxes with the same bones.
  • Key features: a slim torpedo, deepest a third of the way back (depth 0.3 of the length), a narrow waist, a deeply forked tail with red tips; silver flanks, olive-grey back, white belly, a thin darker line along the side, a dark spot at the tail's base, a big eye with a gold ring, small fins with orange tips. Turning, the flank flashes white.
  • Box budget: near model under 40 boxes (the tilapia has 105; 40 barbs near a player), far model under 8.
  • Bones: body, head, tail (two pieces, so the swim can bend it), back fin, breast fins.
