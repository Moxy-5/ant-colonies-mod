Bull_Shark: the brief

Step 1 of the workshop: agree the size and shape before any model. Measured from references/inspiration/bull_shark.png.

Bull Shark (*Carcharhinus leucas*) (M9 river zones & savanna life; Fish family — heavy river-going apex shark, 2026-11-22). Based on the user's inspiration sheet (references/inspiration/bull_shark_sheet.png, scale-matched ortho views in references/inspiration/bull_shark.png).

  • Size & variations (the user's call, 2026-11-22): Base 1.0 grown model = 5.5 blocks long (88.0 model px at 16 px = 1 block), built right in the middle of the user's planned adult size spread (5.0 blocks at 0.91× up to 7.0 blocks at 1.27×; juveniles/pups ~0.45–0.65×). At base 5.5 blocks: total height ~2.40 blocks (38.4 px from pectoral tip to 1st dorsal tip; torso depth ~1.35 blocks / 21.6 px), total width across swept pectoral fins ~3.05 blocks (48.8 px; muscular shoulder width ~1.15 blocks / 18.4 px).
  • Cube size & Minecraft look: Built at 16 model px = 1 block using crisp 2-px stepped-voxel cross-sections (matching the heavy Minecraft concrete/terracotta/stone look of the sheet) mounted on a 7-bone articulated spine with overlapping collars so the shark swims in a heavy, seamless S-wave with zero gaps.
  • Key features: blunt broad rounded bull-shark snout; deep muscular shoulder hump rising into a large, broad triangular 1st dorsal fin; long broad downturned swept pectoral fins; small 2nd dorsal, paired pelvics, small anal fin, and tall asymmetric heterocercal caudal fin (long upper lobe, sturdy lower lobe); 5 deeply recessed vertical gill slits per flank; underslung wide jaw with 3D triangular white teeth, dusty-pink gums/palate, and tongue; small cold black eyes with white pixel glint and pale nictitating-membrane eyelids that flick shut on a bite; jagged mottled dark-slate-to-off-white countershading with dusky fin margins.
  • Box budget: Large animal budget 400 boxes (body_plan.py); target ~170–230 boxes.
  • Bones: root, body (chest → torso → rear → peduncle → tail_base → tail_fin), head (jaw, tongue, lid_left, lid_right), dorsal_1, dorsal_2, anal_fin, pectoral_left/pectoral_right, pelvic_left/pelvic_right, and prey socket inside the mouth.
