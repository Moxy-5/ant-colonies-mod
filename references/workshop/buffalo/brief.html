African Buffalo (*Syncerus caffer*) — Model Brief

  • Mod & Family: savanna (antcolonies:buffalo) | Family: upright_four_legs
  • Target Size (Savanna Scale): 3.80 blocks long (60.8 model px, nose tip to tail drop; ~3.62 blocks nose to rump) × 2.24 blocks tall (35.8 model px at the shoulder withers hump) × 1.28 blocks barrel width (20.4 px) / 2.22 blocks horn span (35.6 px tip-to-tip).
  • Preserves the exact Side turnaround aspect ratio (W/H = 1.697) and Front horn-span-to-height ratio (0.992).
  • Fits our Savanna ecosystem right alongside the approved Male Lion (3.24 b body × 2.00 b tall × 1.14 b wide), Hippo (4.84 b body × 2.00 b tall × 1.98 b wide), and Steve (1.80 b tall — buffalo withers tower slightly above Steve just like the reference's scale silhouette!).
  • Voxel Grid & Box Budget: 16 model px = 1 block; Large-animal budget = 400 boxes (plain 3D shape targets ~165–210 clean boxes, leaving ample headroom for Stage 3 detail).
  • Key Silhouette Features:

1. Fused Central Horn Boss & Sweeping Handlebar Horns: Massive pale-taupe ridged central helmet boss split by a crisp vertical forehead crease, sweeping outward and downward below eye-level before curving boldly upward and inward to dark tapered tips (~1.00 H span).

2. Heavy Shoulder Withers Hump & Deep Brisket: Pronounced muscular stepped ridge peaking at 1.00 H over the forelegs, stepping down to a level muscular mid-back (0.92 H) and deep chest/brisket (0.27 H) with clean belly clearance (0.35 H).

3. Low-Slung Blocky Head & Rectangular Muzzle: Heavy brow overhang with stern recessed eyes, broad dark rectangular snout stepping cleanly forward with wide nose-pad and nostrils, firm lower chin, and wide slightly drooping leaf ears tucked right beneath the horn arch.

4. Sturdy Pillar Legs & Cloven Hooves: Straight chunky forelegs (0.43 H outer stance span), angled muscular hind thighs stepping into sturdy vertical cannons, and broad dark twin-toed cloven hooves planted flush at y = 0.00.

5. Hanging Tufted Tail: Slender 3-bone tail hanging down the rear rump to mid-hock with a chunky diamond brush tassel.
