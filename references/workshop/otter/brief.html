Otter: the brief

Step 1 of the workshop: agree the size and shape before any model. Measured from references/inspiration/otter.png.

African River Otter (*Aonyx capensis*) (M9 river life; Upright Four Legs + Aquatic Extras family, 2026-11-21). Based on the user's AI inspiration sheet (references/inspiration/otter_sheet.png, ortho views extracted to references/inspiration/otter.png).

  • Size (the user's call, confirmed 2026-11-21): 2.6 blocks long (41.6 model px at 16 px = 1 block, drawn scale 1.0 — +0.4 blocks = 6.4 px added into the sleek arched otter torso between hips and shoulders) × ~1.0 block tall at the head crown (16.0 px; gentle arched back ~12.3 px) × ~0.60 blocks wide (9.6 px across shoulders, cheeks, and hips). Pups ~0.55 scale.
  • Cube size & smoothness: built at 16 model px = 1 block using smoothly pitched spine and tail bone chains (hips → waist → ribs → chest → neck → head and 5-segment tapering tail_1..tail_5) with clean 3-tier rounded cross-sections — smooth sinuous otter arch without staircase blocks or noisy micro-voxels.
  • Key features: sleek arched muscular otter torso and thick swept cream throat/chest bib with speckled brown mottling; broad flat-crowned skull with wide cream cheeks, small rounded dark ears with dark hollow inner cups, chunky dark charcoal-brown nose pad with nostrils, crisp Minecraft eyes with eyelids, and 3 pairs of thin 3D 2-segment drooping white whiskers (fanned smoothly outward and downward); 4 sturdy short mammalian legs darkening into broad flat 3-fingered clawless paws; thick muscular tapering tail; internal mouth + carry socket for caught fish/crabs.
  • Box budget: medium animal budget 250 boxes (body_plan.py); built at ~100–120 boxes.
  • Bones: root, body (hips, rump, waist, ribs, chest, neck, head), ear_left/ear_right, eye_left/eye_right, lid_left/lid_right, whisker_left/whisker_right, jaw, tongue, carry, tail_1..tail_5, and four 3-bone legs (leg_fl/fr/bl/br: upper, lower, paw).
