Distichodus: the brief

Step 1 of the workshop: agree the size and shape before any model. Measured from references/inspiration/distichodus.png.

Six-banded distichodus (Claude's design from the user's photo, 2026-11-22; the user: "make the six banded distichodus", "you design it", with the photo distichodus_photo_side.png). The river's big plant-eating fish.

  • Length: made 37 model pixels long, snout to tail tip (the barb 30, the tilapia about 28), drawn in the game at 0.8 blocks grown (my pick: a bit longer than the tilapia's 0.6); each fish 0.85 to 1.15 of that.
  • Cube size: one model pixel; the texture 2 pixels a model pixel, so the six bands are painted, not built.
  • Key features (from the photo): a deep, flat-sided body, deepest at the back fin (depth about 0.3 of the length), the back rising behind a pointed head; a small mouth at the snout tip; a big eye with a red ring; six dark slate bands, straight up and down and evenly spaced, on a peach-gold body (the photo's fish is turned and tilted; the sheet is made level, the user's word) (the first up the nape behind the gill cover, the last a narrow ring at the tail's base); a silver-pink head; a cream belly; a tall swept-back red back fin, a deep forked red tail, red-orange belly, anal and breast fins, a small pale fin on the back near the tail.
  • Box budget: under 70 boxes (a few near a player at once: its own group, cap 8); no far look needed.
  • Bones: body, head, tail (two pieces so the swim can bend it), back fin, breast fins, belly fins.
