Barb_Fry: the brief

Step 1 of the workshop: agree the size and shape before any model. Measured from references/inspiration/barb_fry.png.

Barb fry (Claude's design, 2026-11-20; the user: "lets get started on designing ... the baby silver barb"). Plan: docs/plans/prey-fish-and-frogs.md, step 2. Sheet: tools/young_sheets.py.

  • Length: made 12 model pixels long, drawn in the game at 0.08 blocks as it hatches (my pick: the fry look for its first day, then the grown barb's model drawn small, as real fry look like little adults within days).
  • Cube size: one model pixel (a little finer than the grown barb's: 0.0067 blocks against 0.0083).
  • Key features: a glassy, see-through body (my pick for the game: drawn see-through), two big dark eyes, the silver swim bladder and the gold gut showing through, a row of tiny dark dots along the back, the tail spot just starting, the fins still clear folds, a small forked tail.
  • Box budget: under 12 boxes (up to 30 fry near a breeding school).
  • Bones: the grown barb's names (body, head, tail_stalk, tail_fin, fin_left, fin_right), so its swim, turn and scatter clips can drive it.
