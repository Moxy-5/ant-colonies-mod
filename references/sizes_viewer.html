Insect size viewer

Back row: every savanna insect at the size the game draws it today. Front row: the new sizes, set by the slider (how long a Matabele major is), with every other insect sized from its real length so they keep their real proportions. On the right: a raid column at the new size, single file with a body length between ants, and two majors sharing one block (the yellow square). Grid lines are one block apart. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom. The "eye" cameras use Minecraft's own view (field of view 70, a standing or sneaking player's eye height).
