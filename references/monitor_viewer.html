Nile monitor viewer (step 4: the animations)

Built fresh from your picture and the outline, now painted: dark scales, yellow spot rows and tail rings, a cream belly and throat with grey bars. "Plain colours" shows the bare shape again. The tail, neck, face and legs are rounded and curve smoothly. The body is blocky on purpose. The clip buttons play its animations: walking, standing about, the tongue, swimming and the hiss. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
