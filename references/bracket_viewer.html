Heart Rot on the Trees

Heart rot fungi eat a tree's core while it stays green. They get in through a wound, and their hard woody brackets on the bark show the rot is well along. Here are new looks to pick from: the bracket at each age, brackets in tiers, bark dusted brown by their spores, and the wound where the rot got in, beside today's game bracket.
