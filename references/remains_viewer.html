Remains viewer

What is left of a kill, in four stages from left to right: fresh, half-eaten, picked over, bones. Meat in Minecraft's raw-meat style, no blood. The catfish and tilapia are the game's own fish models, cut open, at their game sizes (2 and 0.6 blocks); the cow is a Minecraft-style cow. Each lies on its side. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
