Matabele ant viewer

The Matabele ant with the head from your picture, in two colours (A black, B the picture's brown-grey), each as a big major (left) and a small minor (right), the heads hanging slanted down, the majors' the most; every face painted with pixels of one size; the "profile" buttons look at each major straight from the side. In front, the game's own insects at their game sizes and a player's height. The grid lines are one block apart. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide. The sliders change only the Matabele sizes.
