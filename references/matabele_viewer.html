Matabele ant viewer

The Matabele ant in black with five new heads, A to E, remade from nothing from your photos (the list under the buttons says what each is), each as a big major (left) and a small minor (right), the heads hanging slanted down, the majors' the most; the "face" buttons look each major in the face, the "head" buttons at its head from the side, the "profile" buttons at the whole ant from the side. In front, the game's own insects at their game sizes and a player's height. The grid lines are one block apart. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide. The sliders change only the Matabele sizes.
