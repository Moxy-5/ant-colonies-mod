Nile tilapia viewer

The grazing fish in three looks, A to C, swimming over a silt bed with the game's own algae. Its body is a chain of bones (head, front, middle, rear, tail stalk, tail fin), so it bends in a wave when it swims, the tail the most, and its side fins flutter; turn the swim off to see it stiff. "Graze" sends the three down to peck the algae; "school" shows seven swimming round together. A block and a player's height stand behind for size. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
