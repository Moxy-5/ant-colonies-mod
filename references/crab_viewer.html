River crab viewer

The river's clean-up crew, built from your picture at half the first model's cube size: a spiny mottled shell, tall black eyes, big claws with cream fingers (the right one bigger) and four orange-brown walking legs a side. One crab stands as in your picture, one scuttles sideways along the bank, one rears up with its claws raised, one picks algae off the river bed and puts it in its mouth, which opens, and one cracks a snail: the game's own crack clip, the snail on the bed first, then the claws lift its shell to the mouth, squeeze till it gives, pick the meat out in two trips, and the broken shell is left lying. A tilapia (0.6 blocks), a player and a block stand by for scale. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
