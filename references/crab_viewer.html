River crab viewer

The river's clean-up crew, built from your picture: a spiny mottled shell, black eyes on stalks, big claws with cream fingers (the right one bigger) and four orange-brown walking legs a side. One crab scuttles sideways along the bank, one rears up with its claws raised, one picks at the algae on the river bed. A tilapia (0.6 blocks), a player and a block stand by for scale. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
