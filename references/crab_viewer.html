River crab viewer

The river's clean-up crew, built from your picture at half the first model's cube size: a spiny mottled shell, tall black eyes, big claws with cream fingers (the right one bigger) and four orange-brown walking legs a side. One crab stands as in your picture, one scuttles sideways along the bank, one rears up with its claws raised, one picks algae off the river bed and puts it in its mouth, which opens. A tilapia (0.6 blocks), a player and a block stand by for scale. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
