River snail viewer

Four cute river snails to pick from, made for looks (no realism): A Garden spiral, B Ruby ramshorn, C Striped steeple and D Mossy nerite, side by side on a silt bank. Switch them between crawling, grazing algae and hiding in their shells. A river crab, a tilapia, a player and a block stand by for scale. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
