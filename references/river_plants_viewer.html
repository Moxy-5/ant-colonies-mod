Reed beds and hippo grass for the savanna's rivers and oxbows (docs/plans/papyrus-and-hippo-grass.md): reeds 2 to 3 blocks over the water in thick beds, in water 1 or 2 deep or on mud at the waterline, for fish to hide in; hippo grass floating out 1 to 3 blocks from the bank, rooted in clumps at the edge, through its life: young shoots creeping out, grown, flowering, then dying back to the bank (straw brown, breaking up) and growing out again. Only on the slow mid and near-coast rivers (none on the fast far-inland ones) and round oxbows. For scale: the grey box is a hippo (4 long), the olive one a crocodile (7.5 long), the blue one a player (1.8 tall). Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to slide.
