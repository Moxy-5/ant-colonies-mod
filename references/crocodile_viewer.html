Nile crocodile viewer

The river's top hunter, built fresh from your picture: blocky like it but long and low. One crocodile basks on the silt bank with its head raised and its mouth open, one swims with only its eyes, nostrils and back above the water. The game's catfish (2 blocks) and tilapia (0.6 blocks) swim below, a player stands by for scale. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
