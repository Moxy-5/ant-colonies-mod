Nile crocodile viewer

The river's top hunter in three looks, A to C, on a silt bank by the water, with the game's catfish (2 blocks) and tilapia (0.6 blocks) swimming below and a player for scale. Its head, neck, body and five tail bones are a chain, the jaw and each leg's three parts are their own bones, so the poses below are only the model moving: lying on the bank, the high walk, basking with the mouth open, and swimming with only the eyes, nostrils and back above the water. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide.
