**Bullfrog tadpole** (Claude's design, 2026-11-20; the user: "lets get started on designing the tadpole"). Plan: `docs/plans/prey-fish-and-frogs.md`, step 4. Sheet: `tools/young_sheets.py`.

- **Length:** made 18 model pixels long, drawn in the game at 0.15 blocks (the plan's pick).
- **Cube size:** one model pixel (0.0083 blocks, the barb's own).
- **Key features:** near black (real bullfrog tadpoles swim in dense black schools); a flat oval body wider than tall, small eyes high on its sides; the mouth underneath at the front, a dark beak with pale lips (it rasps algae); a speckled grey belly with the coiled gut showing; a muscular tail about 1.6 times the body with clear speckled fins above and below, the upper fin starting where the body ends. On day 3 the hind legs grow at the tail's root (own bones, hidden until then); the front legs come out last, as it leaves the water as a froglet.
- **Box budget:** under 30 boxes (a school of 8 to 12, local limit 12).
- **Bones:** body, tail_1, tail_2, tail_3 (so the wriggle can travel down it), leg_left, leg_right (day 3).
