Floodplain Trees

Drafts of the three trees for the savanna floodplain: the fever tree, the sycamore fig and the doum palm. Nothing here is in the game yet. Every tree is made from one seed; "Another tree" shows how much they differ. The little figures at the foot are for size: a player and a Matabele major (half a block long). For scale, the umbrella thorn in the game is about 34 blocks tall and 44 wide.
